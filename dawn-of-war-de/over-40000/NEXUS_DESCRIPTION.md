# Nexus Mods Description — Over 40000 (Dawn of War DE)

## Short Description (max 250 chars)
Over 40000 — a silly power-cheat for Dawn of War: Definitive Edition. Huge population/vehicle caps, 40001 starting resources (variant), and every squad scaled to 5x models. Balance gets weird. Fun only.

---

## Full Description

**This is a fun / meme mod. It is NOT balanced — by design.** Turn everything
up to 11 and enjoy the chaos. Expect silly outcomes:

- An **Imperial Guard Sentinel is now a 5-model squad** and can shred targets in
  seconds.
- A Space Marine Scout squad fields 10 models immediately and 20 at full
  reinforce.
- Terminators, warbosses and other "limited to 1" units can be built
  indefinitely.
- Population and vehicle caps are **40001** for every race and every campaign.

That kind of thing. Some factions become hilariously overpowered. That is the
point. If you want a fair fight, this is not the mod for you.

## What it does

| Feature | Scope |
|---|---|
| Population & vehicle caps → **40001** | All races, all 4 campaigns |
| **Resource cheat variant:** start with **40001** requisition & power, ×10 income | Human player only |
| Every squad's model count → **×5** (initial & max reinforce), per-model build cost counter-scaled so totals stay roughly vanilla | All races, all campaigns |
| Unlimited build limits (Terminators, Dreadnoughts, warbosses…) | Dark Crusade & Soulstorm |

## Two variants (choose ONE to install)

1. **over-40000-v0.1.3** — full cheat: starts you with 40001 requisition & power
   and ×10 income, plus the 5x squads and caps.
2. **over-40000-v0.1.3-normal-resources** — same 5x squads / caps / unlimited
   limits, but **normal resources**: no starting 40001, no income boost. You still
   field armies of 5-model Sentinels, you just have to earn them normally.

The two files are identical except for the resource cheat.

> Single-model campaign variants (e.g. the Wraithlord / Dreadnought honor guard
> units) are intentionally **not** scaled to 5x — spawning 5 of them at mission
> start would freeze the game. Regular single-model vehicles (Sentinels,
> Rhinos, Predators…) still scale to 5x.

## Installation (Vortex, recommended)

1. Install the **DoW DE game extension** for Vortex (`vortex-ext-game-warhammer40kdawnofwar-*.zip`) from this mod's Files tab, if you haven't — drag it onto Vortex's **Extensions** tab and enable it.
2. Drag the chosen `over-40000-v0.1.3*.zip` onto Vortex and **Install**.
3. Click **Deploy Mods**.
4. Launch **Dawn of War Definitive Edition** from Vortex (or Steam — loose files are already deployed to the game folder).

**Uninstall:** disable/purge the mod in Vortex. No game files are modified.

## Notes

- The resource cheat is **campaign-only** (single-player missions and
  engine-driven campaign battles). Skirmish / multiplayer keep vanilla
  resources, caps and income.
- Unit-limit overrides are data-wide, so the AI can also field unlimited
  Terminators. Caps/limits apply to AI too.
- Auto-reinforce is a separate companion mod (`Auto Reinforcement`) — install it
  alongside to auto-reinforce your oversized squads.

## Requirements

- Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War – Definitive Edition (Steam)
- Vortex with the DoW DE game extension (recommended)
