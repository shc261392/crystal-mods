# Lasgun Recolored

**For players who prefer the laser weapon aesthetics from Dawn of War and Space Marine.**

## Overview

There is no single canon colour for laser weapons across Warhammer 40,000 media. They vary significantly between the tabletop game, video games (*Dawn of War*, *Space Marine*, *Battlesector*), novels, and other depictions — different artists and studios interpret them differently.

**Lasgun Recolored** provides an alternative aesthetic: it recolours the Astra Militarum Lasgun and related las weapons to a **red tone**, inspired by the laser weapon effects from Dawn of War and Space Marine series.

**This is purely a visual mod.** Gameplay is completely unchanged — all weapon stats, damage values, abilities, and game rules remain exactly the same. You can freely toggle it on or off.

## What Turns Red

| Effect | Before | After |
|---|--:|--:|
| Projectile bolt & its glowing trail | Yellow/orange | **Red** |
| Muzzle flash & flare at the barrel | Yellow/orange | **Red** |
| Impact flash where the bolt lands | Yellow/orange | **Red** |

- **Weapon**: the standard **Lasgun** (weapon ID 7010)
- **Carried by**: **Astra Militarum Guardsmen** — the Cadian Shock Troops and any unit with the standard Lasgun

The Astra Militarum's laser weapons share common firing effects, so other las weapons in the faction (lascannon, laspistol, multilaser variants) also turn red — providing a unified visual style across the regiment.

## Installation

### Via Vortex (recommended)
1. Install the **Warhammer 40,000: Battlesector** game extension for Vortex
2. Download this mod and drag its archive into Vortex, then enable it

### Manual
Extract the archive into your game directory so that files land in `…/Warhammer 40K Battlesector_Data/StreamingAssets/`, overwriting when asked. **Back up the original files first** if installing manually.

## Uninstall

- **Vortex**: disable or remove the mod — the original file is automatically restored
- **Manual**: restore from your backup, or run Steam's "Verify integrity of game files"

## ⚠️ MULTIPLAYER WARNING ⚠️

**This mod modifies core game files.** Although only visual effects (FX colours) are changed, **multiplayer compatibility has NOT been tested and is NOT recommended.**

**If you play multiplayer:**
- **Disable this mod before joining multiplayer matches**
- Using this mod in multiplayer is **entirely at your own risk** — it may cause desyncs, desynchronization warnings, or other unexpected behaviour with other players
- Out of courtesy to other players, only use this mod in **single-player and skirmish modes**

For a safe multiplayer experience, switch to the unmodded game installation or verify your files.

---

## Compatibility

**No conflicts with:**
- Other mods adding new units or weapons (they use different weapon IDs and effects)
- Mods that modify gameplay rules, damage tables, or non-visual systems — this mod only changes firing effect colours

**Potential conflicts with:**
- Any other mod that also recolours Astra Militarum laser weapon effects — both mods would patch the same game files; the last one loaded would override the others

If you use multiple visual mods, load only one at a time.
